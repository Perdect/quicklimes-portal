When something looks wrong

The five things that come up most often.
An uploaded bill went into the wrong register (Sale vs Purchase)

The app decides this by GSTIN. If the bill's GSTIN matches your firm, it is a Sale; if not, it is a Purchase. A company with no GSTIN saved has nothing to compare against, so every uploaded bill is filed as a Purchase.

Fix it once, in Settings → Company profile: click Edit on the firm and fill in the GSTIN (15 characters, e.g. 08BNAPM0488E1Z3). Bills uploaded after that sort correctly.

A red message says "AI reading is off"

The full message names the cause — for example no AI key is configured on the server, or the AI service could not be reached.

Your bills were still read, by the built-in offline reader, and they were still saved. It is less accurate than the AI, so the message ends with check the figures — that is worth doing before you post them.

This is a server setting, not something you can change from this screen. Send us the exact wording and we will sort it.

The eye button won't open a bill I uploaded

If it says the file isn't stored in this browser, the scan was uploaded on a different device or browser. Scans are saved on this device first and copied to the server in the background, so a bill added on your phone may not be on your laptop yet.

Re-upload it on the device you are using now, or open it on the device you uploaded it from.

The same bank line appears twice

This normally means one statement was imported more than once.

  1. Open Bank Reconciliation.
  2. Use the ⋯ menu on the header → Statements to see everything that has been imported.
  3. Delete the statement you imported twice, or use Remove duplicates, which keeps the first copy of each line and drops the rest.

Both actions reverse any ledger entries that were posted from the lines they remove, so your balances stay correct.

A WhatsApp icon is missing next to a customer

That is deliberate. The icon only shows for numbers that can actually receive WhatsApp — mobile numbers. Landlines and STD-code numbers are skipped, because tapping through to "this number isn't on WhatsApp" wastes your time.

If a mobile number is missing its icon, check the number saved against the party — a missing digit or a stray landline is the usual cause.

Everyday tasks

Where things live.
Change your company's phone number or address

Settings → Company profile → Edit. The mobile number you set here prints on your invoices.

The quick way in: your photo, top right → Edit profile. That opens this form directly.

Match bank lines against bills

Open Bank Reconciliation and click a line. The picker opens beside the table so you can still see what you are matching.

If one payment covers several bills, choose Split across bills. Add each bill on the right and set its amount; the panel keeps a running Payment / Allocated / Unallocated total on the left, and will not let you save an allocation larger than the payment.

Add someone to your team

Settings → Team. You will need their name, the phone number they will log in with, and a password of at least 6 characters. Owners can change a member's role or switch them off later.

Switch between your firms

Use the company name in the top bar. Every screen — sales, purchases, bank, reports — reloads for the firm you pick, and uploads are filed against that firm.
