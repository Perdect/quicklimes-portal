FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything lime manufacturers ask us before starting — answered straight.

What is QuickLimes?

QuickLimes is an AI-powered ERP built exclusively for quick lime and hydrated lime manufacturers — GST billing, purchase, kiln production, chunna, labour wages, cashbook, bank reconciliation and real-time P&L in one app, on web and mobile.

Which industries can use QuickLimes?

Quick lime (calcium oxide), hydrated lime (calcium hydroxide), limestone crushing, lime kilns and calcination plants, chuna manufacturing and lime powder units — any lime business that bills with GST.

Does it handle GST invoicing?

Yes — GST-compliant invoices on your letterhead with HSN codes and vehicle numbers, output tax and input credit tracked, GSTR-ready registers exported for your accountant.

Can it read my old paper bills?

Yes. Upload a PDF or photo and the AI reader extracts the party, GSTIN, quantities and GST, then files it in the correct register automatically — and it learns from your corrections.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes — the whole app works in Hindi and English. Staff use it in Hindi; your accountant exports English GST reports.

How much does it cost?

Start with a 1-month free trial, no card required. Then Starter is free forever, Pro is ₹999/month (₹9,999/year), and Enterprise for multi-plant groups is ₹2,499/month.

Can I move from Excel or Tally?

Yes — import parties and bills from Excel/PDF, keep exporting accountant-ready data anytime. Most plants are fully live within a week.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. QuickLimes runs on phone, tablet and computer, and installs like an app on your phone — bill from the office, check dispatch from the yard.

Can I run more than one firm?

Yes. Enterprise runs multiple firms and plants from one login — each with its own GSTIN, books, letterhead and reports, switchable in one tap.

Is my data safe, and can I take it out?

Your books belong to you. Data is scoped per company, exportable at any time, and never sold or shared.
