How does the free trial work?

Every new account starts with a 1-month free trial of the full product — no credit card needed. When it ends, keep using Starter free forever, or upgrade to Pro or Enterprise.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term lock-in — upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like, and your data remains exportable at all times.

What counts as a “company”?

A company is one firm with its own GSTIN, books and letterhead. Enterprise lets you run several firms and plants from one login and switch between them instantly.
